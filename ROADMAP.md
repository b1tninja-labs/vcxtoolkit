# vcxtoolkit

The frame, the USB open, one live `GetInfo` exchange, the image-header compare, the page records, the on-device license info, the license retrieve, and the firmware catalog have been checked. The update sequence is drawn in `docs/protocol.md`. `plan_firmware` names those steps and `hold` refuses the ones that would be sent. The pass-through copies and the ISO 15765 PCI names are in `docs/reads.md`. They are not sent. The open row is a brake-module identity read, with no security seed and no write. `winhive` and `iso9660` are not this project. The crate does not write a license or a firmware image to the device. `docs/tool-boundary.md` says this crate owns the dongle and `spskit` owns the GM wrapper contract. Neither package implements `j2534bridge.dll`. The `vcxtoolkit-j2534` workspace member (`j2534/`) is our own SAE J2534-1 pass-through DLL that hosts the dongle for stock J2534 apps; it is a native driver (`docs/j2534.md`): the channel bring-up lives in the library as reusable `Channel` primitives and the cdylib wires the `PassThru*` exports to them, with `unsafe` confined to its FFI boundary; it writes no registry and synthesizes no vehicle request (the app owns the data). The design is grounded directly in SAE J2534 (2002), `sae:j2534-2002` in `primarysource`.
